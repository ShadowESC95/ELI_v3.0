## 4. License key & activation

Paid ELI v3 builds use **license-key activation**:

1. **Purchase** — you receive a license key (email/receipt/account).
2. **Activate** — enter the key in the installer or first-run setup (or via `eli --activate-key …`).
3. **Bind** — the key is associated with your licensed machine(s) or account per tier.
4. **Offline use** — ELI is local-first; activation is designed to work without sending your
   conversations or documents to the cloud. Only license validation traffic is used where needed.
5. **Transfer** — deactivate on the old machine before moving a single-seat license (fair-use
   hardware refresh is allowed; abuse may void the license).

Trial builds may run without a key for a limited period or with reduced features. Source builds
from GitHub under PolyForm Internal Use do **not** include a commercial license key.
