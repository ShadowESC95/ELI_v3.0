## 1. Grant

On purchase, you receive the licence in §3 of the master terms for ELI Creation Station, for the
version line and number of machines or seats above, for personal, hobby and commercial work as your
tier allows. ELI Creation Station is a separate ELI Product: an ELI v3 licence does not include it
unless your receipt says so, and its licence does not include ELI v3.

## 2. What you make

2.1 **Your outputs.** As between you and us, what you make with ELI Creation Station is yours, to the
extent the law allows; we claim no rights in it. We do not warrant that an output can be protected
by copyright, or that it does not resemble or infringe someone else's work. **[to confirm]**

2.2 **You are responsible for what you publish.** Check every output before you publish, sell or
deliver it: facts, names, likenesses, trade marks, platform rules and the law where it is seen.

## 3. Models and their licences

3.1 **Models are third-party software.** Each model keeps its own licence (master terms §9). The
models ELI Creation Station downloads for you are only those whose licence lets a paid product pass
them on and lets their output be used commercially.

3.2 **Models you bring.** A model you add yourself is used under its own licence, which you accept,
not us. ELI Creation Station records that licence's class with everything the model makes and warns
before you export an output whose model's licence limits its use; you remain responsible for
respecting it.

## 4. Marking of AI output

ELI Creation Station writes a machine-readable mark into the images, video and audio its models make,
and keeps a record of every output's models, so that AI-generated content can be recognised, as the
EU AI Act asks of a provider (Article 50(2)). Where you publish AI-generated or manipulated content
that resembles real people, places or events, a duty to disclose it may fall on you as deployer
(Article 50(4)). **[to confirm against the AI Act's text and dates as amended]**

## 5. What you must not do with it

5.1 Make sexual content involving a minor, by any description or likeness.

5.2 Make sexual or intimate imagery of a real person without their consent, or content that depicts
a real person saying or doing what they did not, presented as genuine.

5.3 Clone a person's voice without that person's recorded consent.

5.4 Use it to infringe someone else's rights, to deceive, or to harass.

## 6. ELI in the studio

Following a project's folder and reading the screen are off until you turn them on, happen on your
machine only, and are not sent anywhere. If your screen may show other people's information (a
client's files, a call), you are responsible for having the right to let ELI read it.
