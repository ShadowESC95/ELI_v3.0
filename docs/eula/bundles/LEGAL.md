## 1. Grant

On purchase, you receive the licence in §3 of the master terms for ELI Legal, for the version line
and number of machines or seats above. ELI Legal is a separate ELI Product: an ELI v3 licence does
not include it, and its licence does not include ELI v3.

## 2. What ELI Legal is, and is not

2.1 **Not legal advice.** ELI Legal prepares material for a named human to review. It does not give
legal advice, and it is not a substitute for a qualified lawyer.

2.2 **No decisions.** ELI Legal stops before the decision. It renders no verdict, award,
recommendation on the merits or settlement terms, and you must not present its output as if it did.

2.3 **You remain responsible.** You remain fully responsible for any advice, filing, submission or
decision you make, and for meeting your professional and regulatory obligations. Before relying on
any output you must check every source, citation and quotation it gives, and check that any authority
it cites is still good law.

## 3. Regulation

3.1 **High-risk classification.** Our own assessment, set out in ELI Legal's documentation, is that
ELI Legal is a high-risk AI system under the EU AI Act (Article 6(2) and Annex III, point 8(a)). That
documentation is an aid, not legal advice.

3.2 **No conformity assessment yet.** ELI Legal has not completed a conformity assessment. Until we
state in writing that it has, you must not use it in any way that requires a conformity-assessed
system.

3.3 **Your obligations as deployer.** If you deploy ELI Legal, you are responsible for the
obligations the law places on deployers, including human oversight, record-keeping, and informing the
people affected where that is required.

## 4. Validation and limits

4.1 ELI Legal has been evaluated on synthetic matters only. It has not been validated on
representative real matters. Before you rely on it in practice, you must set accuracy thresholds and
confirm they are met for your own matters.

4.2 The results depend on the legal sources you load into it. The accuracy and currency of those
sources, and your licences to use them, are your responsibility.

## 5. Confidentiality and data

5.1 ELI Legal processes your matters on your own machines and sends nothing to us.

5.2 It does not encrypt data at rest. You must store matter data on an encrypted volume and control
who can reach the machine.

5.3 You are responsible for client confidentiality and privilege, and for data protection
obligations as the controller of the personal data in your matters.

## 6. Server and multi-user use

ELI Legal includes a server for use inside your organisation. Keep it on an internal network. The
number of people who may use it is the seat count on your Licence Key. Using it to provide services
to people outside your organisation needs written permission from us (master terms §7).
